About the project

Mindscreen wraps a regression model built on the Student Social Media and Mental Health Impact dataset. The model reads habits like daily screen time, phone unlocks, sleep and physical activity, then predicts the mental health score recorded for similar students.

The prediction is made by a Random Forest regressor (R² ≈ 0.88 on held-out data) served by a FastAPI backend. Nothing on this page is simulated: every score comes from the trained model.

How it works

  1. 1

    Describe your day

    Fill in the form with your background, platform habits, study, sleep and activity.

  2. 2

    Send to the API

    Your answers are checked in the browser, then posted as JSON to the FastAPI /predict endpoint.

  3. 3

    Model predicts

    The saved pipeline scales numbers, encodes categories and the Random Forest returns a score.

  4. 4

    Read the dashboard

    See your score on the dial, how your habits compare with the training data, and what the model weighs most.
